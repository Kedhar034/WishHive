Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes! WishHive is 100% free to use for unlimited Hives and Wishes.

Can friends see what I buy?

If you mark a wish as "Fulfilled", the owner won't know WHO bought it until they check the app, keeping the surprise alive!

Does it work with any store?

Yes, you can add links from any website. Our AI tries to fetch the details, or you can add them manually.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. You control who sees your Hives: Public, Friends Only, or Specific People.
