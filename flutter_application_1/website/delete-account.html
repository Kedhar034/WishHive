Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to permanently delete your WishHive account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be erased.

What will be deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, photo, username)
  • All Hives you have created
  • All wishes and items you have added
  • Your friends list and connections
  • All account activity and history

How to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to our support team at wishhive@outlook.com
  2. Use the subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address registered with your WishHive account
  4. Our team will process your request within 7 business days

Your data will be fully deleted from our servers within 30 days of your request being processed.
