Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: March 2025

🛡️ WishHive is committed to creating a safe environment for all users and has a zero-tolerance policy for any content that endangers children.

Our Commitment

WishHive strictly prohibits the creation, sharing, or distribution of any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors. We are fully committed to complying with all child safety laws and cooperating with law enforcement authorities worldwide.

Prohibited Content & Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on WishHive:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Content that sexually exploits, grooms, or endangers minors
  • Any communication designed to exploit or harm children
  • Sharing personal information of minors without consent

Reporting Mechanisms

WishHive provides in-app reporting tools that allow users to flag any content or behavior they believe violates our child safety standards. All reports are reviewed promptly.

  • Users can report any post, user, or content directly within the app
  • Reported content is reviewed and removed if it violates our policies
  • Accounts found violating child safety policies are permanently banned

Compliance & Reporting to Authorities

WishHive complies with all applicable child safety laws, including mandatory reporting requirements. Any identified CSAM is immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional/national authorities in accordance with applicable law.

Enforcement

  • Violations result in immediate account suspension and permanent ban
  • Content is removed immediately upon verification
  • Evidence is preserved and reported to law enforcement as required

To report child safety concerns or for compliance inquiries, contact our designated safety officer:
