# 🐝 WisHive
# 

### Your Smart Wishlist & Personal Shopping Hive

**WisHive** is a Flutter-based mobile app designed to make shopping simple, smart, and social.  
It lets users save, organize, and track products they wish to buy — all in one place.  
Built with **Flutter** and powered by **Firebase**, WisHive redefines how users plan their purchases and discover what others are loving.

---

## 🚀 Vision

We often find great products online — for a vacation, festival, or just future plans — but forget them later.  
**WisHive** acts as your *personal digital wishlist companion* that not only remembers your desired products but also makes it easy to revisit and buy them at the right time.

Soon, WisHive will also feature a **community marketplace**, where users can showcase their favorite products anonymously, allowing others to explore and get inspired — without revealing personal identities.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🛍️ **Smart Wishlist** – Save any product link from Amazon, Flipkart, or other stores directly into WisHive by using the “Share” option on your phone.
- 🔗 **Affiliate Integration** – Every saved product is automatically converted into a verified affiliate link for smart monetization( Verified users can showcase their products with their personalize affiliate links andshare the full Hive).
- 🕹️ **Clean UI & Animations** – Built with Flutter for a smooth, minimal, and engaging experience.
- ☁️ **Firebase Powered** – Secure and scalable backend for authentication, data storage, and link management.
- 🧠 **Marketplace (Coming Soon)** – Discover trending wishlist items shared by others, completely anonymous and safe.
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** – Get notified before events or offers on your wishlist items.

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|-------------|
| **Frontend** | Flutter (Dart) |
| **Backend** | Firebase Firestore, Firebase Auth, Firebase Cloud Functions |
| **Affiliate Conversion** | Cuelinks API (for multi-platform monetization) |
| **State Management** | Provider / Riverpod |
| **Hosting** | Firebase Hosting (for web version - upcoming) |
